# conclusion

AI is not going away and will reshape our societies. This is just the beginning.

You are the first generation to have to make sense of it

- learn to work, learn to learn
- what makes us humans
- what's real, what's not

What I learned in this course
- no work, no joy: my voice, my writing, imperfect as it is, is worth more than AI polished content because it is not perfect.
-I can use AI to help me make the connections in my head, to get a clearer picture of things. Something AI can't do for me.


My predictions

- a renewed interest for human only creation
- learning institutions (school, higher ed, ...) will adapt painfully. and it will take time.
- different type of work, not less work for humans.


- read books
- use AI to learn and work
- don't use AI to speak in your name. (except corporate emails maybe).

Thank you
